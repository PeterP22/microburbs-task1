Method

  1. Hook. Start from the cultural idea that run clubs are where people meet now.
  2. Primary-source club setting. Pick a real social run whose organiser publishes the route: She Runs starts in the CBD and finishes in Surry Hills.
  3. Microburbs metric definition. Read the Surry Hills lifestyle score, then look up what the metric measures on the livability-map endpoint.
  4. Editorial review. Strip every claim we could not source cleanly, and hold the exact score as PROVISIONAL after the public-page mismatch.
  5. Produced reel (PRESENT, approval PENDING). Remotion, ElevenLabs narration (Jono), original music, Higgsfield AI-illustrated b-roll, ffmpeg encode. Map instead of a wrongly generated cathedral. v11 omits the disputed 94. Files are at repo root; final user approval is still PENDING.

What worked

  • The romance hook gives a reason to watch before any number appears.
  • One metric with its official definition is easier to say accurately than a stack of stats.
  • Quoting the metric definition kept the "going out" framing tied to the source.
  • The public ranking cross-check caught the 94 vs 91.0 clash before a headline number went into a video.

What did not work

  • Exact score as a settled fact: API 94 and public page 91.0 disagree. Dropped from videos. Kept here as PROVISIONAL.
  • Age share of young adults: age arrays in the sample were truncated, so the figure could not be verified. Dropped.
  • Cafe and park counts: totals conflicted between endpoints. Dropped.
  • Rent angle: an earlier rent story was rejected; its figures are not used.
  • "Singles" framing: "not married" is not "single". Dropped.
  • Club demographics: suburb characteristics are not attendee characteristics, and we have no attendee age, sexuality or relationship data.
  • Generated cathedral still: v11 uses a map instead.

Held-out check

This is a manual cross-endpoint definition check, not a predictive held-out dataset. We took the number from one endpoint (/lifestyle/livability) and checked its meaning against a separate endpoint (/lifestyle/livability-map), so the narration describes what the score measures rather than what we assumed. That definition check is why the reel must not say "94%" or "94 venues".

A second, independent value check against the public ranking page failed: 94 vs 91.0, with no date or version sufficient to reconcile. The contradictory score is unresolved, not validated. Recommendation: omit the exact score from the videos.

No independent audience or engagement validation yet. No view counts. No dating-outcome measurement and no causal evidence.

Limits

  • The lifestyle score is PROVISIONAL. Do not treat 94 or 91.0 as known current/correct.
  • No independent audience or engagement validation yet. No view counts.
  • No dating-outcome measurement and no causal evidence. Run-club participation and dating odds were never measured. Microburbs is not claimed to change romance outcomes.
  • The b-roll is AI illustration, not footage of She Runs or any real club.
  • Clips labelled withoutMicroburbs (friendzone) and withMicroburbsAPI (mutual flirting) are a fictional AI dramatisation, not a promised outcome, and not documentary evidence of a club.
  • Club schedules are as the publisher describes them, not observed.
  • Rights and organiser clearance are pending; we do not claim all licences are cleared.
  • The reel is hand-made. A repeatable data-to-story format across topics and cities is future vision, not something we built. There is no working content engine in this repository.
