## Five candidate patterns, not verified viral trends
1. **Neighbour versus neighbour:** “Same city. Two very different price tags.” Two adjacent outlines, matched price measures, one contextual contrast.
2. **Budget constraint:** “Where does your budget actually go further?” Same dwelling type and reference period, reveal then explain.
3. **Expectation reversal:** “The expensive suburb is not the winner on this measure.” Name the precise measure rather than claiming ‘best’.
4. **Guess then reveal:** “Which suburb has more renters?” Two cards, brief pause, verified percentages and denominator.
5. **Map contrast:** “This boundary changes the picture.” One geographic comparison without implying the boundary causes the difference.

These are editorial hypotheses. No verified example post URLs were obtained, and none are fabricated.
